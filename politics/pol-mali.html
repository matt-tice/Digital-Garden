Geography

Huge: Largest country in west Africa, and eighth-largest on the continent.

Landlocked:

Sahara (North)

Sahara desert: Covers 65% of the country. Shifting sand dunes, gravel plains, rugged rocks; hot, barren.

Desertification: The Sahara is slowly expanding southward each year, destroying farmland.

Nomads: Indigenous groups like the Tuareg

Sahelian (Center)

Semi-arid: Dry, sandy plains, sparse shrubs, baobab trees.

Bandiagara Escarpment: A massive cliff. The Dogon people live there.

Hombori Tondo: A huge mountain with a flat top

Niger river: The most important geographic feature of Mali. Creates the Inland Niger Delta, which is incredibly fertile. It is an oasis in the middle of Mali. Used for transporting goods, fishing, and growing crops.

Sudanic (South)

Wet: Regular rainfall. The vast majority of the population live here.

Withdrawl from ECOWAS (2025)

Mali grew frustrated with sanctions and criticism towards its non-democratic governance, and decided to withdraw from the Economic Community of West African States on January 29, 2025

Alliance of Sahel States

Mali, Niger, and Burkina Faso joined together to form the AES (Alliance of Sahel States) to support each other in dealing with jihadist attacks.

Joint parliament (2026)

The goal is to eventually form a single confederation, with free trade, shared market, and potentially a single currency.
