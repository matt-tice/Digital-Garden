* SSH-Keygen

#+begin_src bash
ssh-keygen -t ed25519 -C "your_email@example.com"
#+end_src

ssh-keygen will generate a public-private key pair:
- private: =id_ed25519=
- public: =id_ed25519.pub=

the =-C= flag is for /comments/
- this just says to tag the keys with your email, so if you have multiple accounts, you remember which account the key belongs to
