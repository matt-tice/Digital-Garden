Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE)

The idea is to use rotations to represent the positions of words in text

  • and to use radial distance as a measure of importance

It's kind of weird that you have to use angles for distance, but it's basically because the way that transformers work, you interpret the dot product as a measure of attention

The Kernel Trick

Aliasing

The different angles need to rotate at different speeds, so you don't end up with orthogonal vectors, or two vectors that are meant to represent different words, but are the exact same location (this is called aliasing)

The problem is that if your angular increments are set ahead of time, if the context window gets really big, eventually vectors can get too close to ones that have already been seen

  • partitioning a circle into finitely many parts will always give you a way to get back to where you started
  • then the lcm of the different rotation increments needed to get back to 0 degrees will reset the whole vector

Another issue is that if the intervals are too small, we get floating point errors

  • the computer can't keep up the precision, and therefore can't tell things like whether you're at word 1 or 2
