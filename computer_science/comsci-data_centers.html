Summary

Google

Uses tensor processing units, rather than GPUs. TPUs have slightly lower energy usage.

Since 2015, google has been making custom TPUs for things like powering Google Search

  • TPUs are strictly designed for matrix multiplication, as opposed to GPUs, which have control logic and memory fetching
  • once you start a TPU process, it never reads or writes to external memory

Uses

  • AI / machine learning
  • Cloud computing
  • Cybersecurity
  • National Security and defense
  • Financial clearing and payment systems
  • Healthcare data and diagnostics
  • Industrial automation and logistics

Cloud Computing

  • Web hosting
  • Corporate databases
  • Email
  • Video streaming (Netflix)
  • Software as a service

Resource Usage

Relatively little. CPUs are mainly used. Single racks use about 10-15 kW

Because of the lower power usage, they can often get away with air cooling. Alternatively, there's mild evaporation-assisted cooling.

  • 2 L / kWh

AI Training

Processing pentabytes of data

Resource Usage

Very high

Uses hundreds of thousands of GPUs, which can pull up to 1.2 kW per chip 150-230 kW per rack

Air cooling is ruled out by the high heat, so you need direct-to-chip liquid cooling

  • 5 L/Kwh

AI Inference

Running a trained model to answer prompts

Resource Usage

Moderate per request, but massive at scale

  • individual prompts use about 0.25 - 2 Wh (approx. an LED light bulb being run for 1.5 - 12 minutes)
  • but billions of queries per day

10 to 50 basic queries can evaporate 500 mL of water

Water usage is 9L / kWh

Images take 5-30 times more energy (about as much as charging a smartphone from 0 to 100%). And 20-100 times more water (about 500 mL)

5 sec of video generation can take a further 30-150 times more energy (running a dishwasher for a cycle)

  • on the low end, this is about as much as watching one hour of netflix in 4K

Usage Limits

The free tier of something ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini puts you around 10-80 Wh / day (about as much as a fridge uses running for 1 hour)

  • for Gemini, it's about 12-24 Wh / day for basic chats; if you try to skirt around this with images or documentd-contexted prompts, you'll get capped around 50 Wh / day
  • this equates to around 26 mL / day for maxing out text prompts

Location

Data centers are meant to couple into business or consumer-use infrastructure. This makes them bunch together, leading to heavy loads on the power grids in those locations.

Northern Virginia has the world's largest concentration of data centers
