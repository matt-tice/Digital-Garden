MTHFR

Your body takes folate from food and uses the MTHFR enzyme to turn it into L-methylfolate, which can then be used to make all sorts of neurotransmitters, including serotonin

Methylfolate becomes BH4 (tetrahydrobiopterin)

Receptors

Serotonin has 14 different receptor types. Most of them are G-protein coupled

We measure the affinity for serotonin to bind to a given type of receptor by that receptor's dissociation constants Kd (equilibrium constant) and Ki (inhibition constant). The lower the constant, the higher the affinity.

Serotonin receptors are located all throughout your body.

Serotonin Transporter (SERT)

Sits on the wall of the sending neuron (the one releasing the serotonin into the synaptic cleft). When serotonin is released, it bounces around in the cleft; if it hits a receptor, it binds to it. If it hits a transporter, it gets reabsorbed

The serotonin that gets attached to a receptor eventually falls off and starts bouncing around again in the cleft, eventually either getting reabsorbed or binding to a receptor.

SSRIs

When you take an SSRI, it immediately targets the synaptic cleft. The presynaptic neuron is covered in a protein called SERT (serotonin transporter).

SERT sucks used serotonin out of the cleft, to recycle it. SSRIs bind into the SERT protein, inhibiting it.

  • the serotonin then pools inside of the synaptic cleft.

Activating the serotonin receptors in the gut triggers nausea

Sertraline is designed to inhibit the dopamine transporter slightly.

Micro-Rewards

Though SSRIs end up inhibiting phasic dopamine spikes, if you focus on getting lots of tiny dopamine releases, instead of one big one, the SSRI won't necessarily be able to block that.

You can use this to maintain focus throughout the day.

Cold Showers

Actually stimulate 250% dopamine

5-HT1A Autoreceptors

When there's too much serotonin hanging around, these autoreceptors tell the neurons to stop releasing more serotonin.

Over time, if you maintain high serotonin, the 5-HT1A receptors will be dissolved and the effects of the higher serotonin will finally take hold.

BDNF

Serotonin stimulates brain-derived neurotrophic factor, which in turn stimulates neurogenesis in the hippocampus.

This in turn allows the DMN to change its focus to be less on negative rumination.
