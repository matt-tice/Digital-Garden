MTHFR

Methyl-tetra-hydro-folate (active methylfolate) reductase

A reductase adds electrons to a molecule. MTHFR adds electrons (reduces) methylene-folate to turn it into methyl-tetra-hydro-folate.

The MTHFR enzyme takes folate (vitamin B9) and uses it to make neurotransmitters.

  • folate is made into methylfolate, which then can be used to make dopamine, norepinephrine, and serotonin

On the other hand, unmethylated folate is used to build new cells and repair DNA

  • it's a kind of healing supply

C677T Mutation

40-50% of European and Hispanic people have at least one copy of this variant.

Fascinatingly, it helps protect against malaria. Malaria works by hijacking your folate cycle, in order to replicate itself
