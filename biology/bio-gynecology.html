Menstrual Fluid

Often called "blood", it's in fact more general. Half of it is blood, the rest is mucus, vaginal secretions, and endometrial tissue

Usually about 10–80ml / month

Blood clots are common during menstruation

Plasmin, contained in the endometrium tends to inhibit blood clotting

Iron

The amount of iron lost in menstral fluid is typically very small. Most women with iron deficiency have a gastrointestinal disease
