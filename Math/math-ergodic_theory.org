* Summary

Ergodics is also called *measurable dynamics*
- you work with dynamical systems f: X \to X, in the category of measurable functions
- the point is to study *long-term behaviour*

All completely separable metric spaces with a Borel measure are isomorphic to a finite or infinite interval in R union with a countable number of isolated points
