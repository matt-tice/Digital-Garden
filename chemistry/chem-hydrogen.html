Burning Hydrogen

Hydrogen can be burned at a high temperature, like fossil fuels. But unlike them, it only produces water when burned.

This makes it a wonderfully green source of heat (and therefore electricity, through producing steam for generators).

Creating Hydrogen

It's very difficult to find natural hydrogen.

So most hydrogen is produced industrially.

Grey Hydrogen

Produced using fossil fuels.

Steam Methane Reforming (SMR)

Methane is reformed into hydrogen and CO2 is released into the atmosphere.

  • the amount of CO2 produced is 10x the amount of H2 made in SMR.
  • this is the most common method of producing hydrogen.

Blue Hydrogen

Produced using natural gas alongside carbon capture.
