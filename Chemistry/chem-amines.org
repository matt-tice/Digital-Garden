#+title: amines
#+filetags: Chemistry:Biology:

* References 

* Summary 

Amines are organic compounds that contain *C-N bonds*

1. Primary amines: (R-NH_2)
        These have an amino group

2. Secondary amines: (R_2-NH)

3. Tertiary amines: (R_3-N)


Amines are usually created from [[id:90693255-6072-44fd-91bb-b688d6d3f1fb][ammonia]]

* Types


** Primary

Methylamine, amino acids, tris(hydroxymethyl)aminomethane

Methylamine is used to make meth by combining it with phenylacetone (aka P2P)

** Secondary

Dimethylamine, diphenylamine


** Tertiary

Trimethylamine (smells like fish)

Decaying fish produce trimethylamine
