Coal

Canada has actually managed to phase out coal to a large degree All provinces except SK and NS are set to phase out coal by 2030

Coal plants have been generally transformed into gas ones, and emissions have fallen by 58% since 2005

It's cheap to cut down on methane, and technologically simple Methane is also 80x more potent than CO2 as a greenhouse gas

Canada has reduced methane emissions by 31% since 2015

Legislation

2017:

Revenue-neutral carbon pricing

Unfortunately, after COVID, when inflation surged and people got really worried about affordability, carbon pricing became unpopular People like Poilieve liked to attack it

It's now abandoned

2021: Net-Zero Emissions Accountability Act

Binds government to produce, monitor and report on climate strategies at regular intervals, in a way that works towards net-zero emissons by 2050

2024: Amendment to the Competition Act

Requires companies to substantiate their environmental claims Making greenwashing effectively illegal

2025's Budget might roll this back

Subsidies

Canada has some of the highest fossil fuel subsidies in the world

This mainly appears through

  1. Tax deductions
  2. Discounted royalty rates
  3. Lost-cost funding
