Summary

Minerals are inorganic solids with ordered crystaline structure. They're the building blocks that form rocks

Criteria

Naturally occurring: So we don't count things like lab-grown diamonds or synthetic rubies

Inorganic: Cannot be made by living organisms. Therefore wood, pearls, and coal are not minerals

Solid: Must be solid at room temperature and pressure. Liquid water and mercury thus don't count. Technically though, naturally occuring glacial ice is a mineral!

Definite chemical composition: Every single sample of a fixed mineral has the exact same chemical elements in the same ratio.

Crystaline: The atoms are arranged in a periodic pattern.

Earth's crust

Oxygen/sulfur: Nearly 47% of the crust (by weight) is oxygen and sulfur. This is why we see so many minerals which are made up of (at least in part) those two elements.
