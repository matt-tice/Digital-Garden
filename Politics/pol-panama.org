** Panama Canal

Businesses have paid up to $4M to move boats through the *Panama Canal*, since the Strait of Hormuz is closed
- usually to get through the canal, you reserve passage at a flat rate
- but you can pay extra to cross without a reservation
- in that case, passage is granted to the highest bidder
