* K-Armed Bandit

One of the simplest problems

A slot machine with K levers
- you're allowed to pull one
- they each have different potential award amounts
- the task is to maximize the reward

  
* Temporal Difference Learning

You assign /internal rewards/ at all of the hidden layer steps (steps before the final step)

The further the internal step is from the final step, the more its reward is decreased.
So for instance, the final reward might be 100, the second last step gets 90, the third last gets 85, etc.
