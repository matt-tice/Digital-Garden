* Summary 

30% of solar radiation is immediately reflected back into space
The rest is absorbed by the land, sea, and atmosphere

The heat is eventually released as infrared radiation
The temperature of the earth is determined by how much of the heat is retained

If incoming energy is greater than the energy lost, we heat up


Greenhouse gases absorb infrared radiation and reradiate it in *all directions*
- So while some of it goes out into space, it's less than initially came in

Sea water absorbs more heat than ice, so as the ice melts, the earth gets even warmer

When plants die they release GhG
