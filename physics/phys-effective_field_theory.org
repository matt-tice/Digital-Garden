* Summary 

EFT is the state-of-the-art in dealing with physical systems that have *many different energy scales* occuring at once.

You typically use perturbation theory at high energies and [[id:ae907339-c8f3-4e8e-ba95-bd1e52a1d0f4][lattice QFT]] at low energies

* Heavy Quarkonium

The main example of a multi-scale system in QCD
+ It's a bound state of bottom or charm quarks with their antiparticles

EFTs can be obtained for HQ by integrating out degrees of freedom related to high energies

Two types of EFTs are non-relativistic QCD (NRQCD) and potential non-relativistic QCD (pNRQCD)

** pNRQCD

At the scale of the heavy quarkonium binding energy

HQ decays into a lighter quarkonium by releasing EM radiation
