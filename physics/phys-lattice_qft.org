* Summary 

Lattice QFT is a numerical method for non-perturbative calculations
It computes correlators and matrix elements

The idea is to discretize spacetime to force finite degrees of freedom.

[[id:cb195e7f-59ad-4e34-9ce2-c0dbd122b43d][Effective field theory]] is required in lattice QFT, because you need a way of figuring out whether your discretization makes sense. EFT can predict finite size corrections


* Applications

Hadronic physics at zero temperature

QCD at finite temperature
+ QGP phase

Highly improved staggered quarks


* Results

Abinitio QCD+QED calculations of proton-neutron mass splitting

Strong constraints on CKM matrix elements

Chiral transition temperature
