Interaction

When an electron interacts with an element in a lattice, it briefly localizes, delocalizing its momentum state

When the electron and phonon interact, they become entangled

  • or perhaps more precisely, the interaction term in the Hamiltonian is an entanglement operator

Voltage

Voltage is essentially the scalar potential ϕ, where E =  − ∇ϕ. Say we place a fixed voltage across a length of wire L. This creates an interaction term in the Lagrangian.

Assume ϕ =  − Ex. As the electron field propagates from one end of the wire to the other, the potential energy drops. The lost energy is converted to kinetic energy, as the electron increases momentum.

Resistance

Consider electrons propagating through a lattice.

The lattice atoms vibrate, which gives off phonons (typically infrared photons).

When electron fields propagate through the lattice, they interact with the photon fields, undergoing scattering.

When electrons emit phonons, they introduce heat into the system

Electrons in a lattice are surrounded by virtual phonons, forming a polaron.
