* Summary

Electronic devices require a medium through which electrons can flow
The two main ways are
1. creating a (near) vacuum
2. creating a (near) perfect crystal

You also need a *source of charged particles*
1. heating a cathode to liberate electrons
2. inputting an impurity with free electrons into the crystal

You then move the electrons with an electric field   

*Currents* are very unfortunate concepts
- they're defined to travel in the opposite direction from the flow of electrons.
- So in my analysis, I'm just going to replace ordinary currents with their negatives, because it conceptually makes more sense to look at how the electrons move, than the current itself, which may or may not correspond to something physical.
