* Bank of Canada

** 1934

/Bank of Canada Act/ establishes the central bank
It is originally publically owned

** 1935

The bank's original goal is to maintain a fixed exchange rate

** 1938

The bank becomes a Crown corporation


** 1962

Again the bank's goal becomes to maintain the exchange rate

** 1975

The goal becomes to fight inflation by controlling money growth

** 1991

The goal is to target a specific inflation rate

** 1995

An inflation rate of 2% is agreed upon by the Bank and the government
