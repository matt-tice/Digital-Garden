#+title: annuities
#+filetags: Economics

* References 

* Summary 

 

** Life Annuity

Provides a guaranteed lifetime income
- basically you pick some amount, say $100,000. You pay that amount, and it gets paid back to you in installments over the course of the rest of your life.
- if you live longer than expected, you still get the payments, and end up making more money

** Term-Certain Annuity

Provides guaranteed income for a fixed period (term)
Even if you die, your beneficiary will receive the rest

The problem is that in this case, since the term is fixed, you could live on past the end of the term, and stop receiving money

** Variable Annuity

Your money is invested
You receive both a fixed and variable income
- fixed is usually lower than what you'd get with a non-variable annuity
