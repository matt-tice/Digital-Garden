#+title: human capital
#+filetags: Economics:Marxism:

* References 

Ibbotson, Milevsky, Chen, Zhu, Lifetime Financial Advice


* Summary 


Human capital is the *present value of the anticipated earnings over the remainder of your life*

Education, therefore, is an investment into human capital

Over the early part of our lives, we develop human capital. When we enter the workforce, we translate this capital into financial capital. As life goes on, we reduce our human capital, but (ideally) increase our financial capital

Even at the end of our lives, we still have human capital if we get things like CPP

Human capital is usually devoid of much risk, compared to stocks
- this is why people recommend you try to put a lot of equity into your early portfolio, since you have such a safe human capital
- as you lose human capital, you increase your risk, and so ideally should mitigate that by putting your financial capital into less risky things
