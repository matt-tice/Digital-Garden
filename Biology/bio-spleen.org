#+title: spleen
#+filetags: Medicine

* References 

* Summary 


A blood filter: removes old erythrocytes and holds a reserve of blood in case of hemorrhagic shock
It also recycles iron
Essentially a large lymph node





* Location

Left side of the body, attached to the stomach
Left upper quadrant

* Diseases

** Hypersplenism

*** Splenomegaly

Enlargement of the spleen
