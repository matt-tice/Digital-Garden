* Summary 


Erythrocytes: red blood cells


** Macrophages

White blood cells of the innate immune system that *engulf* and *digest* pathogens

*** Granuloma

What you call a bunch of macrophages engulfing a foreign substance


* Tests


** Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Measures all the different blood cells

1. Red blood cells
   carry oxygen
2. White blood cells
   fight infections
3. Platelets
   stop bleeding by clotting
4. Hemoglobin
   iron-rich protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen
5. Hematocrit
   amount of whole blood made up of red blood cells
6. Mean corpuscular volume
   size of red blood cells


** Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate (ESR)

Sedimentation rate: time for red blood cells to settle at the bottom of a test tube

[[id:89db38a0-0c02-4ab0-aee5-a9b526431852][inflammation]] causes red blood cells to clump together, making them fall to the bottom of the tube faster
