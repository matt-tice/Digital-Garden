* Summary 

The goal of a commercial power plant is to *produce heat*

Coal, gas, and nuclear plants all do this. The difference is in how the heat is produced

** Coal

Coal is burned, producing heat

** Gas

Gas is burned, producing heat

** Nuclear

Fission is performed

* Basic Design

** [[id:37e5c94c-38d5-4fde-9db7-41e8b7cf170a][Nuclear Power]]

1. Nuclear fission produces heat
2. The heat is picked up by a coolant
3. The coolant cycles around, and goes to a steam generator
4. The steam generator picks up the heat, through its secondary coolant
5. The secondary coolant evaporates, turning into steam that turns a generator
6. The generator produces electricity
7. The steam is sent into a condenser, which cools it, either using cooling towers, or a large water source like a lake
8. The cooled steam becomes water and is sent back into the secondary coolant area
