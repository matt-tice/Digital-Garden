* Summary 


Normally, in order to stimulate the economy, the central bank will lower the interest rate

But what if the interest rate is already low? Conceivably we could raise the interest rate forever, but we seem to have a hard cut off at 0%

** Quantitative Easing

Meant to increase inflation

The central bank buys bonds from the government (loaning the government money)
- Because they buy the bonds, the price of bonds goes up (higher demand) and their yield goes down
  - Now other people are more incentivized to spend and invest in stocks rather than buy government bonds


Buying 5-year bonds in QE will affect interest rates on 5-year fixed-rate mortgages, for instance

Buying 10-year bonds would affect more long-term things

** Signals

When the central bank does QE, it tells people that they're going to keep their target (policy) interest rate low for a long time
- This provides security to busineses and banks (and consumers) in terms of the economic trajectory of the country
