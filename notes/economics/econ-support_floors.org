* Summary


Support floors are *regions*, rather than unique price points.

=End of day:= Prices frequently dip below support intraday. But buyers can step in and push the price back up to the floor. This isn't a true break in the floor --- to actually break the floor, you need the price to be below the floor at the *end of day* 

=Volume:= If the stock closes below the floor on *above average volume*, that all but confirms that sellers are going to drag the price lower.
