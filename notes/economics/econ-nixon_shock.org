#+title: nixon shock
#+filetags: Economics:Shock

* References 

* Summary 



Nixon talks to a bunch of people at the Federal Reserve and his cabinet, and decides to suspend conversion of USD into gold
- this essentially ends the [[id:de94bb6f-d100-4bd8-8ffc-41da48057610][Bretton-Woods system]]

Nixon announces 3 goals:
1. lower unemployment
2. lower inflation
3. protect the US dollar from price-gougers

He then introduced 10% [[id:78b7b66d-1752-4425-bc22-21e4c5392d94][tariffs]] on imports
- this was designed to get other countries to be hurt enough that they would agree to appreciate their currencies
- its primary effect was to make people angry
