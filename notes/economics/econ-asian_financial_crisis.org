#+title: asian financial crisis
#+filetags: :Economics:Crisis:

* References 

* Summary 

Thailand's currency was the bhat


* History

** 1991

Recession in the US
- Central bank has to lower the interest rate a lot
  
South East Asian countries had a much higher interest rate and were of keen interest to investors
- American money starts flooding in to SEA

As the US raises its interest rate back up, its dollar increases in value
- But many countries had their currencies pegged to the US dollar
- This caused their currencies to also increase in value, which in turn made them less attractive to investors
  - Another way to think of it is, essentially SEA rates rose with the US, even if their central banks didn't do anything
  - As a consequence, deflationary pressure is put on SEA

** 1993

Thailand creates new financial industry in Bankok, with limited regulations

** 1997

Thailand's foreign exchange reserves run out
- they can no longer exchange bhat for dollars, and so have to stop pegging to the USD
