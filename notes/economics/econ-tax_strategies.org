* Summary

Managing your taxes often comes down to not what you have, but *where you put it*

Different asset classes have different tax rates, so you'd obviously like to place things in the most beneficial boxes

* Sole Proprietorship

Capital losses become *business losses*
- meaning it can be used to offset *any income*, including dividends
- it can also be carried forward/backward in time

  
** Deductions

Capital Cost Allowance for computer hardware

** Home Office

Square-footage percentage of rent
- whatever percentage your desk / office takes up in the apartment
- you can deduct this from
       1. rent
       2. heating/hydro
       3. home insurance

 
*** Exlusive Use

In order to deduct, you need the space to be exclusively used by you and your business
