* Summary 

An *investment bank* facilitates large-scale financial transactions between corporations and financial markets.

Such a bank normally has a lot of connections, which it uses in order to sell off securities that corporations are looking to part with


*** Private Placement

A company creates a bunch of new shares to sell. They go to an investment bank and offer them the chance to be the person who gets to sell these shares.

If the bank agrees, they purchase some amount of the shares, and then use their connections to find buyers. The bank can sell these shares at a premium and pocket the difference
