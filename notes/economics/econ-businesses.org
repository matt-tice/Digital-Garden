* Sole Proprietorship

The owner has
- sole responsibility in making decisions
- gets all profits
- absorbs all losses
- does not have separate legal status from the business

You don't register as a company
- it's a classification you apply on your T1 form

** Tax

One of the interesting trade-offs when it comes to business income v.s. capital gains, is that if you sit in a low enough tax bracket, the extra 50% inclusion rate becomes negligible
- if you make under the Basic Personal Amount, you're already tax free
- so you can utilize the advantages of business income without the tax penalty
