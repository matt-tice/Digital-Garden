* Market Open

** Margin Wash

If a stock has been dropping a lot in price, the market open can be heavy with margin call sale orders and panic sells
- the stock hits its low in the first 15-30min


*** Institutional

Institutional investors rarely do large market orders at open, because they're bound by *execution mandates* to minimize slippage


** Liquidity Vacuum

In a low-liquidity environment (like the TSX.V) price can swing wildly.

If the aggressive selling stops, the price can rocket quickly if there's a reason to buy
