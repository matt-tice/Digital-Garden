* Summary 


** Organization of Arab Petroleum Exporting Countries

Northern Africa and the Levant countries:
- Algeria, Bahrain, Egypt, Iraq, Kuwait, Libya, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Syria, United Arab Emirates, Tunisia

Founded by Kuwait, Libya, and Saudi Arabia

Originally places line Egypt and Algeria weren't meant to be included, because their main exports weren't oil

* History

** 1959

*** Mandatory Oil Import Quota Program

President Eisenhower in the US puts a limit on the amount of oil the US is allowed to import
- he doesn't want to be dependent on foreign oil

The US mainly imported oil from Venezuela and Canada

** 1969

At this point the US has a fantastic auto sector, and can't keep up with demand; it's oil production is too slow

** 1971

Nixon puts a [[id:e879adec-7904-4765-8a4b-ef980e09964b][price ceiling]] on oil

** 1972

The OAPEC allows countries that only have oil as a major export, rather than their primary one
- so they add Algeria, Iraq, Syria, and Egypt

  
** 1973


*** Yom Kippur War

Egypt and Syria launch a suprise attack on the Jewish holy day of Yom Kippur (which was also the 10th day of Ramadan)

The US supports Israel and the Soviets support the Arab states

OAPEC decides to reduce production of oil by 5% / month to any country that supports Israel, until Israel leaves all territories it occupied in the [[id:38050f0c-87f8-4634-a52b-d88ebea85e43][1967 six-day war]]
- Canada, Japan, the Netherlands, the UK, US, Portugal, Rhodesia, and South Africa


Over the next year, the price of oil rises 300%
