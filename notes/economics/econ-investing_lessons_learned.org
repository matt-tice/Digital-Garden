Lessons I've learned (often the hard way) since starting to invest and trade in May 2025.

* Margin requirements change

* Some stocks really are predictable

* Use strict stop losses Always

* Don't doubt stock movement

I've spent too many trading days (weeks, months) expecting a correction on dying stocks, or waiting for a price to drop while suffering opportunity cost.



* Save margin space
