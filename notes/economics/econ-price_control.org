#+title: price control

* References 

* Summary 

** Price Ceiling

Fixes a price that you aren't allowed to charge above

Consumers are able to afford the controlled goods more easily

It can be inefficient
- If supply goes down, prices want to go up; if they can't it can lead to a shortage, since the price no longer dissuades people from purchasing

** Price Floor

Fixes a price you aren't allowed to charge below

A *minimum wage* is a price floor
- Friedman argued that under this price floor, companies will just stop hiring unskilled labor
