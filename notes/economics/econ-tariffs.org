* Impact

** External

If the US puts tariffs on Canadian goods, that makes it harder for US citizens to buy Canadian
So it reduces the demand on Canadian goods

Consequently, it can weaken the economy of Canada, lowering employment, causing deflation, etc.

Tariffs on Canada introduce *uncertainty*, which always drives down investment

Also tariffs hit *productivity*, because they force resources to get reallocated in a non-optimal way; productive export industries might contract, and labour/capital shift to less efficient sectors


*** Currency

In 1971 Nixon put a tariff on all imports
- to basically attack all other countries, reducing their imports. His goal was to get them to capitulate to appreciating their currencies with respect to the dollar.

** Internal

On the other hand, because tariffs increase prices for consumers, it actually causes inflation in the US when the US applies tariffs to other countries

So to do a tariff basically amounts to deflating someone else's economy at the cost of inflating your own
