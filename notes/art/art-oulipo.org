* Summary

=1960s:= The movement was started by french mathematicians and wriers. 

=Title:= The full title is /Ouvroir de Littérature Potentielle/ 

=Constraints:= The Oulipians believed that true creative freedom comes from strict, intentional constraints on your work

=Bad freedom:= Too much freedom leads to predictability and writer's block 

* Ideas

** N+7

Replace every noun in an existing piece by one seven words down in the dictionary

** Lipogram

Banning a particular letter of the alphabet in a work

** Snowball

Each line is one word long, and each of those words increases by one letter each time

** Palindromes

Reads the same forwards and backwards

** Prisoner's constraint

You aren't allowed to use any letters that have descenders or ascenders: for example b or d

That leaves aceimnorsuvwxz
