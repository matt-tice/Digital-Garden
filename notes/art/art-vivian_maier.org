* Summary

Worked as a live-in nanny

Took 150k photographs around new york city, but died in 2009 in near-poverty, completely unknown

She never intended to monetize her art; her whole life was structured around acquiring resources to pursue photography
        - as a live-in nanny she had essentially no expenses
        - she spent all of her money on photography equipment
        - when her family's farm was sold, she used the money to fund travel around the world.
        - she negotiated for a private bathroom, which she converted into a darkroom, which cut down on processing fees
        - eventually she switched to colour photography and wasn't able to convert it at home; not being able to pay processing fees to have it done professionally, she chose to just leave photos undeveloped, while continuing to shoot them
