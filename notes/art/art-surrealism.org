* Summary

* Techniques

** Automatism

The goal is to write faster than the conscious mind can think

** Cadavre Exquis (exquisite corpse)

A bunch of artists get together, each writing a word or drawing a section of a body on a piece of paper. They then cover what they made and pass the paper to the next person, who adds to the work without seeing what the previous person did

** Le Hasard Objectif

** Frottage

Marx Ernst laid paper over textured surfaces like floorboards or leaves, and rub a pencil over it. He would use this as a baseline for the full piece

** Coulage

Pouring molten material into cold water, letting it instantly freeze into a random shape, and then interpretting it

** If/when

Two players: A and B

A writes a conditional clause starting with /if/ or /when/ 

Player B doesn't see what A wrote, but writes a consequence
