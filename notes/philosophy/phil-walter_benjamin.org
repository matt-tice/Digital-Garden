* The Work of Art in the Age of Mechanical Reproduction


=Aura:= The /here-and-now-ness/ of a work of art. It keeps the artwork *distant, sacred, and untouchable* 

=Mass reproduction:= Destroys the aura. Photography, printing presses, film, allow artwork to be copied; you can take a photo of the Mona Lisa and look at it in your living room. The mystique - the aura - is killed, and art is given to the people.

Traditional art positions itself as a higher figure, and trains viewers to subjugate themselves to authority. Mass-produced art is a *tool for communicating working-class reality* 

=Distraction:= Unlike Adorno, who thought that consuming art passively was a serious problem, and reinforced passivity, Benjamin believed that it allowed people to break free from authoritarian control and view the work as a *distracted critic*
