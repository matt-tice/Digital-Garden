* Summary 

** Definition

Given an alphabet A, with free monoid A^*,
a *formal language* L with alphabet A
is a subset of A^*.


* Types

Each kind of language corresponds to a certain kind of [[id:f88b47e5-7b81-4fc5-bf34-2a6290bd457a][automaton]]
