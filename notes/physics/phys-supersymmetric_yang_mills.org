* Summary

\(\mathcal{N}\) counts the number of supersymmetry generators

Supersymmetry essentially means that we have operators \(Q_{i}, i =1, \ldots, \mathcal{N}\) called *supercharges* which map bosons back and forth to fermions


=Spinors on curved space:= Fermions are very difficult to treat in curved spacetime --- as spinors, their values depend on the orientation of the curvature, which makes taking path integrals over them very hard

In \(\mathcal{N} = 2\) Yang-Mills, your connection \(A\) has to have two superpartners, which are both fermions. Witten in his [[id:2d82b7e0-703b-4e07-9630-ab21e88e7304][topological quantum field theory]] reexpressed the equations in terms of differential forms, which allowed him to solve it
