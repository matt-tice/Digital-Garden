* Summary

Renormalization arises from the issues with the [[id:61a71036-e556-4e60-8927-f11ef0d00729][interaction picture]].

Because we can't square a delta distribution, we have to find a way to get rid of them, if we want to deal with interactions.

We can try to mollify them in such a way that when the interaction turns off
\[
g \to 0
\]
we recover the delta distribution. But it turns out that it will *depend on our choice of mollifier*.

If we mollify the interaction term, we get *distribution splitting*. If we mollify the propagator we get *non-local regularization* 

* Distribution Splitting

It turns out that any two choices of mollified interactions differ by a sum of delta function derivatives. The coefficients of this sum (and thus the exact mollified field interaction can only be determined experimentally)

* Non-Local Regularization

If you mollify the spacetime delta distribution, the problem is that you break locality --- the smeared-out fields can interact outside of the light cone
