** Yau's HQFT

He uses the W construction to homotopify both the underlying spacetime and the algebras at the same time.

He ends up with an adjunction from his HQFTs and A_\infty-algebras, induced by any map from Assoc
- this basically says you can associate a free A_\infty-algebra to any region of spacetime in his model.
- The downside is that you don't have much control over these A_\infty-algebras: since they come from an adjunction, they lose a lot of info about your theory
