* Summary

Not every physical quantum field theory can be acquired from taking a classical Lagrangian and quantizing.
        * this tends to happen for *strongly coupled QFTs*, because quantizing a classical theory usually involves taking a non-interacting classical field theory, and using the coupling constant as a perturbation of interactions.
        * this is related to *quantum parameter locking* 

Moreover, there are some quantum field theories that have no Lagrangian at all! 
        * \((2,0)\) superconformal field theory in 6D. It describes strings and membranes moving in 6D. It looks at the *self-dual three-form field strength* 
        * the main problem is that if you try to write out a Lagrangian, the kinetic energy term blows up
        * *This where the *bootstrap method* comes in --- you start with the observables and a number of assumptions like unitarity, locality, and conformal symmetry, and together they're enough to let you work out the correlation functions
