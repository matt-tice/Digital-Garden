** Dialectical Materialism

Einstein's theories were in direct contradiction with Stalin's mandated science of dialectical materialism.

=$E=mc^{2}$:= The equivalence of mass and energy was seen as making Einstein's theory *non-material*, and hence, bourgeois.

=Relativity:= The idea that time and space depend on relative frames of view was antithetical to the objectivism of Soviet science.

=Spying:= The Soviets actually sent a spy after Einstein --- Margarita Konenkova started a romantic relationship with Einstein after his wife died and had a mission to gather information on Princeton and Los Alamos scientists' knowledge.
