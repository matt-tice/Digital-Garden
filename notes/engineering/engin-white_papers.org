#+title: white papers
#+filetags: :engineering:business:consulting:

* References


* Summary

A persuasive, evidence-based report that outlines
- a complex industry problem
- a specific solution

You can often repurpose the same white paper for things like
- LinkedIn posts
- conference talks
- follow-ups after networking

Start by focusing on 3-5 page papers, focusing on narrow technical friction.

* Structure

** Executive Summary

"Executive", meaning /written for executives/
- concise
- what, why, how

** Context / Background

Why the issue matters right now

** Problem Statement

Clear, objective description

** Proposed Solution

Unique insight

** Evidence / Analysis

Data, case studies, mathematical models

** Conclusion

Professional summary of why this solution is the most logical
