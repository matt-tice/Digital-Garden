* Summary 



* Coal

Canada has actually managed to phase out coal to a large degree
All provinces except SK and NS are set to phase out coal by *2030*

Coal plants have been generally transformed into gas ones, and emissions have fallen by *58% since 2005*



* Oil / Gas

30% of Canada's emissions; the largest share

* Methane

It's cheap to cut down on methane, and technologically simple
Methane is also *80x more potent than CO_2 as a greenhouse gas*

Canada has reduced methane emissions by 31% since 2015

* Emission Caps

AB and SK push back on any emission caps on oil and gas

* Public Transit

25% of emissions. The second-highest

* Mining

* Legislation

** 2017:

Revenue-neutral carbon pricing

Unfortunately, after COVID, when inflation surged and people got really worried about affordability, carbon pricing became unpopular
People like Poilieve liked to attack it

It's now abandoned


** 2021: Net-Zero Emissions Accountability Act

Binds government to produce, monitor and report on climate strategies at regular intervals, in a way that works towards *net-zero emissons by 2050*

** 2024: Amendment to the Competition Act

Requires companies to substantiate their environmental claims
Making greenwashing effectively illegal

2025's Budget might roll this back

** Subsidies

Canada has some of the *highest fossil fuel subsidies in the world*

This mainly appears through
1. Tax deductions
2. Discounted royalty rates
3. Lost-cost funding

** Lobbying

Fossil fuel lobbies are the *most powerful*
- In 2024 alone, oil/gas companies had 1100 meetings with federal officials (more than 4/day)
