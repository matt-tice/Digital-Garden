** Compatibility with a Pairing

A topology on $V$ is called *compatible with the pairing* $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ if the continuous dual is $V^{*}$

** Weak Topology

Let $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle : V \otimes W \rightarrow k$ be a non-degenerate pairing. The *weak topology on $V$,* $\sigma(V,W)$ is the initial topology on all
\[
\langle -, w \rangle : V \rightarrow k
\]
Similarly, the weak topology on $W$ is the initial topology on
\[
\langle v, - \rangle : W \rightarrow k
\]

*** Intuition

The weak topology on $V$ is the weakest topology on $V$ such that
\[
V' \cong W
\].
Similarly, on $W$, it's the weakest topology on $W$ such that
\[
W' \cong V.
\]


** Mackey Topology

Given a dual pairing $\langle \cdot , \cdot  \rangle: X \otimes  Y \rightarrow k$, the *Mackey topology* on $X$ is the strongest topology such that 
\[
X' \cong Y
\].
Similarly, the Mackey topology on $Y$ is the strongest topology making
$Y' \cong  X$

** Weak Star Completion

Let $X^{*}$ be the algebraic dual space. Give it the topology induced by the evaluation dual pairing with $X$:
\[
\langle \phi, x \rangle = \phi(x)
\]

This is called the *weak star topology* on $X^{*}$.

Given a subset of linear functionals $A \subseteq X^{*}$ its closure in the weak star topology is called its  
*weak star completion*

** Mackey-Arens Theorem

A locally convex topological vector space $(V, \tau)$ is compatible with a pairing $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ if and only if 
\[
\sigma(V, V') \subseteq \tau \subseteq \tau(V, V')
\]

where $\sigma(V,V'^{})$ is the weak topology with respect to the evaluation pairing with the continuous linear functionals defined by $\tau$ and $\tau(V, V')$ is the Mackey topology induced by $\tau$.
