** Compatibility with a Pairing

A topology on $V$ is called *compatible with the pairing* $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ if the continuous dual is $V^{*}$

** Weak Topology

Let $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle : V \times W \rightarrow k$ be a non-degenerate pairing. The *weak topology* $\sigma(V,W)$ is the initial topology on all
\[
\langle -, w \rangle : V \rightarrow k
\]

** Mackey-Arens Theorem

A locally convex topological vector space $(V, \tau)$ is compatible with a pairing $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ if and only if 
\[
\sigma(V, V') \subseteq \tau \subseteq \tau(V, V')
\]

where $\sigma(V,V'^{})$ is the weak topology with respect to the evaluation pairing with the continuous linear functionals defined by $\tau$ and $\tau(V, V')$ is the Mackey topology induced by $\tau$.
