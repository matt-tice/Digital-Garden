** Fully Faithful

*** FF Reflects Isomorphisms

#+ATTR_LATEX: :options {FF Functors Refelct Isomorphisms} {fullyfaith-refl-isos}
#+begin_lemma

Let $F: \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}$ be fully faithful. Then if $F(f)$ is an isomorphism, so is $f$.

#+end_lemma
#+begin_proof-lemma

Let $F(f)$ be an isomorphism, so there's an inverse $\phi$. By fullness, there's a $g$ with $F(g) = \phi.$ Then
\[
1 = F(f)F(g) = F(fg).
\]
By faithfulness, we have $fg = 1$. Similarly, $gf = 1$ . Thus $m$ is an inverse. 



#+end_proof-lemma
