* Definitions

** Characteristic of a field

** Field extensions

A field extension is a pair of fields \( k \leq F \), where \( k \) is a subfield of \( F \). The field \( F \) is called an *extension* of \( k. \)

*** Notes

\( F \) is a vector space over \( k \). 

** Degree of extension

Let \( k \leq F \) be a field extension. Then the dimension of \( F \) as a vector space over \( k \) is called the *degree* of the extension, and is denoted
\[
[F : k]
\]


* Theorems

** All ideals are trivial

Let \( k \) be a field. Then every ideal is either \( 0 \) or \( k \). 

*** Proof

If \( I \) is any non-zero ideal, we can find \( x \in I \). Then since \( I \) is an ideal we have \( x^{-1} x = 1 \in  I \), which implies \( I = k. \)


** Characteristic is zero or prime

** Field extension characterization

Let \( k \leq F \) be a field extension. Thne \( [F: k] = 1 \) if and only if \( F \simeq  k \).
