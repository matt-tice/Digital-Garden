* Summary

Two manifolds intersect transversely if they still intersect after a perturbation.

* Definitions

** Transversality of Submanifolds

Two submanifolds $X,Y$ of a finite-dimensional $M$ are said to *intersect transversely* if for all $p \in X \cap Y$, we have

$T_{p} X + T_{p} Y = T_{p} M$

** Transversality of Smooth Maps

** Lie Algebra Approach

We start with a Lie group and pass to the Lie algebra to get the infinitesimal generators

$X_{p}^{*} = \dfrac{d}{dt}|_{t=0} (\exp(t X) p) \in T_{p} M$, where $X \in \mathfrak{g}$.

The set $\{ X_{p}^{*} \}$ is the tangent space to the G-orbit through $p$.

The action of $G$ is *infinitesimally transverse* to the intersection of two submanifolds $A, B$ at $p$ if
$T_{p}A + T_{p} B + {X^{*}_{p}} = T_{p}M$

We can also define transversality of submanifolds in this language. We take the diffeomorphism group to be $G$. The Lie algebra is the space of all smooth vector fields on $M$. Every such vector field generates a flow

$\Phi_{t}: M \rightarrow M$. We view these as our perturbations.

Consider the evaluation map

$ev_{p} : \Xi(M) \rightarrow T_{p}M$ that sends
$X \rightarrow X(p)$.

Now given two submanifolds $A,B$, we take the Lie subalgebras of vector fields tangent to each of them:

$\mathfrak{g}_{A} = \{ X \in \Xi(M) \mid X(q) \in T_{q} A, \; \forall q \in A \}$

We have $ev_{p}(\mathfrak{g}_{A}) = T_{p}A$.

Now transversality is precisely the condition that

$ev_{p}(\mathfrak{g}_{A}) + ev_{p}(\mathfrak{g}_{B}) = ev_{p}(\mathfrak{g})$
