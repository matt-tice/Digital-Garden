** Homological mirror symmetry

There is an equivalence of derived categories:

\[
D^{b} (Fuk(M)) \cong D^{b}(Coh(Y))
\]

between the derived category of the Fukaya category of a manifold \(M\), and the derived category of coherent sheaves on the dual manifold \(Y\)
