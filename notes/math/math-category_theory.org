** Congruences

A congruence in a category is an equivalence relation such that if \(f \sim g\) then \(f h \sim gh\) and \(hf \sim hg\) whenever these compositions make sense.

The quotient category with respect to a congruence is the category with the same objects, but morphisms are equivalence classes under the congruence.
