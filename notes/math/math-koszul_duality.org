** Quadratic Algebras

#+ATTR_LATEX: :options {Quadratic Algebra} {quad-alg}
#+begin_definition
A /quadratic algebra/ is any quotient of the form
\[
A = T(V)/\langle R \rangle
\]
where $V$ is a finite-dimensional vector space and $R$ is a subspace of relations of degree $2$.
#+end_definition
#+begin_defframe

=Symmetric algebra:= Taking $R = \{ x \otimes y - y \otimes x \mid x,y \in V \}$ exhibits $S(V)$ as a quadratic algebra.

=Exterior algebra:= Taking $R = \{ x \otimes y + y \otimes x \mid x,y \in V \}$ exhibits $\Lambda(V)$ as a quadratic algebra.

#+end_defframe


** Koszul Dual Algebra

#+ATTR_LATEX: :options {Koszul Dual} {koszul-dual-alg}
#+begin_definition
The /Koszul dual/ of a quadratic algebra $A = T(V)/\langle R \rangle$ is the algebra
\[
A^{!} = T(V^{*}) / \langle R^{\perp} \rangle
\],
where $R^{\perp} \subseteq V^{*} \otimes V^{*}$ is the orthogonal complement under the pairing
\[
\langle \cdot , \cdot \rangle: (V^{*} \otimes V^{*}) \otimes (V \otimes V) \rightarrow k.
\]

#+end_definition
