** Tangent Spaces (extrinsic)

Let $p \in M \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n}$ be a point on a manifold (defined as a subspace of Euclidean space). Then the *tangent space* at $p$ is 

$T_{p}M = \{ \gamma'(0) \mid \gamma: I \rightarrow M$ for all intervals $I$ and smooth $\gamma \}$
