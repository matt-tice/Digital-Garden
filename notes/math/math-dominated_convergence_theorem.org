* Summary

We want to know if $f_{n} \rightarrow 0$ pointwise implies that 
\[
\lim \int f_{n} d \mu \rightarrow 0.
\]

This is a question of local versus global behaviour. If the dominated convergence theorem fails, knowing about the local data isn't enough to tell us about what happens globally.
