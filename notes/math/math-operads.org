** Symmetric sequence

Let \(C\) be a set. Define \(S[C]\) to be the category whose objects are pairs \((A, f)\), where \(A\) is a finite set and \(f: A \rightarrow C\) is a set map. Morphisms \((A,f) \rightarrow (B, g)\) in this category are maps \(\phi : A \rightarrow B\)  such that 
\[
f = g \circ \phi
\]


A *symmetric sequence* is a functor
\[
F: S[C] \times C \rightarrow \mathcal{V}
\]

*** Intuition

The category \(S[C]\) is really almost the *category of elements* of the presheaf \(\operatorname{Mor}(-, C)\)
