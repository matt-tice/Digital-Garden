* Summary

A foliation is a way to stratify a space: you create a bunch of /layers/ of the space made up of submanifolds. Each layer is called a *leaf* of the foliation

* Definitions

** Foliations

A *foliation of degree $k$* of an $n$-manifold $M$ is a partition of $M$ into connected, $k$-dimensional submanifolds such that for all $p \in M$, there is a local coordinate chart $(x^{i})$ where the leaves are defined by holding the last $n-k$ coordinates constant.
