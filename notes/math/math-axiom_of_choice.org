* Measure Theory

AoC allows you to create unmeasurable sets, like the Vitali set.


Solovay showed that if we assume the existence of an /inaccessible cardinal/, we can keep Dependent Choice and get *every subset of $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ measurable.*

The problem is, this also gives you that every linear operator on a Hilbert space is automatically bounded and continuous on its domain. Consequently, the unbounded position and momentum operators of quantum mechanics don't show up.
