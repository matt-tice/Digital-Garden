#+title: c star algebras
#+filetags: :math:operators:

* References

* Summary

$C^{\infty}(M)$ is a Frechet algebra. Gelfand duality tells us we can reconstruct our space from the commutative C-star algebra of continuous functions.

The smooth functions form a dense subalgebra of this C-star algebra.

The GNS construction says that we take a state and evaluate it on a C-star algebra (and take the closure in the weak operator topology), to get a von Neumann algebra.
