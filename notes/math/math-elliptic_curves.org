* References


* Summary

We're interested in finding rational points on the curve: $x,y \in \mathbb{Q}, E(x,y) = 0$

Elliptic curves are deeply related to [[id:19dac00f-58d5-4457-ab28-8d6a01c69293][elliptic integrals]]


* History

** 1922

Mordell proved that the rational points on any elliptic curve form an abelian group, and in fact this group is *finitely generated*


** 1960s

Hasse-Weil L-functions seem to tell us about the algebraic rank of an elliptic curve

* Definitions

** Rank of an Elliptic Curve

*** Algebraic Rank

The *algebraic rank* is the rank of the finitely generated abelian group determined by the curve.

If $r = 0$ we have a finite number of rational points

If $r > 0$ we have an infinite number of rational points

*** Analytic Rank

*** Problem: Finding the Rank

Currently (2026) there's no algorithm for finding the rank of an elliptic curve
