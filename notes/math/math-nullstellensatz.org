** Weak Nullstellensatz

Let \( k \) be algebraic closed, and take a finite collection of polynomials \( f_{1,} \ldots , f_{m} \in k[x_{1}, \ldots , x_{n}]  \). Then there is a simultaneous solution \( v \) with \( f_{i}(v) = 0 \) for all \( i \) if and only if \( \langle f_{1}, \ldots , f_{m} \rangle\subsetneq k[x_{1} \ldots x_{n}] \) is a proper ideal

*** Proof

Say all \( f_{i}(v) = 0\). Then if \( 1 \in \langle f_{1}, \ldots , f_{m} \rangle \), we would have \( 1 = \sum_{i} f_{i} (x) g_{i} (x) \). So \( 1 = \sum_{i} f_{i}(v) g_{i} (v) = 0 \). Since this isn't possible, we must have \( 1 \neq \langle f_{1}, \ldots, f_{m} \rangle \), so this is a proper ideal.
