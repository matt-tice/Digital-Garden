*** Notes

Quasicategories usually have more than one model-category presentation

** Homotopy Colimits don't satisfy a universal property

** There can be more than one model for a homotopy colimit in a given setting (they're weakly equiv)

This shows up when you're trying to define

$homcolim_{I} hocolim_{J} F \cong hocolim_{I}_{\times_}$

$\alpha : I \rightarrow J$
F: J \rightarrow

hocolim_I F \cdot \alpha  \rightarrow hocolim_J F

hocolim F \cong F(0)

you don't need it to preserve weak equivalence!
