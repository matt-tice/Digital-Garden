* Summary

The Jacobian of a function is a matrix of all of that functions partial derivatives

We often care about the *rank* of the Jacobian. The rank is the dimension of the image of the matrix.

This is because the Jacobian is really the *data of the tangent space* at a given point.

* Definitions

** Jacobians

Let $f: \mathbb{R}^{m} \rightarrow R^{n}$ be differentiable. The *Jacobian of $f$* is the matrix

$Df_{i}_{j} = \partial_i \partial_{j} f$

*** Rank

The *rank* of a linear transformation is the dimension of the image. 

** Immersions

If $\ker Df = 0$ (the Jacobian is one-to-one) then $f$ is called an *immersion*.

** Submersions

If $Df$ is surjective, then $f$ is called a *submersion.*
