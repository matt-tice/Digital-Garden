* Summary

These things show up when you want to work with differential equations that have *singularities* 

Ordinarily, when working with [[id:a3ec7f7f-0799-4e6d-980d-e14c5c7617f9][vector bundles]] and connections, if there are no singularities, our solutions form a *local system* --- the transition maps are constant.

The singularities force us to chop up our space into different regions; each one of these regions forms a local system, and then we stitch them all together with a *constructible sheaf*.

=The problem:= Constructible sheaves don't have Poincaré duality. You can restore it by replacing a single sheaf with a *chain complex of constructible sheaves*, called a perverse sheaf
