* Summary

In the classical setup, we start with a commutative C-star algebra of continuous functions on a manifold: $C_{0}(M)$. We pass to a dense subset, like $C^{\infty}(M)$, and use this as our space of test functions.

We can generalize this by replacing our C-star algebra with a von Neumann algebra, and taking a dense subalgebra as our test functions.
