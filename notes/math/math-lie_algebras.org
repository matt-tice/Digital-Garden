** Ado's Theorem

Every finite-dimensional Lie algebra over $\mathbb{R}$ has a faithful finite-dimensional representation 

$\rho: \mathfrak{g} \rightarrow \mathfrak{gl}(N, \mathbb{R})$

So you can view every finite-dimensional real Lie algebra as a Lie algebra of matrices.
