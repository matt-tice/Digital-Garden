* Summary


Hodge theory is about the relationship between the global structure of a manifold, and calculus on that manifold.

=Goal:= Given a de Rham cohomology class, how do you find a nice representative?

Once you have the Hodge star, you define an inner product on differential forms by 
\[
\langle \alpha , \beta \rangle = \int \alpha \wedge \star \beta .
\]

A form \(\omega\) is called *harmonic* if 
\[
\Delta \omega = 0.
\]

** Dirichlet energy functional

\[
\int \omega \wedge \star \omega
\]


This is essentially the same type of thing as the Yang-Mills action.
