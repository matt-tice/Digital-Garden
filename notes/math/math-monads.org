** Monads

A *monad* on a category $C$ is a triple $(F, \eta, \mu)$, where $F: C \rightarrow C$ is an endofunctor, and 

$\mu \circ \mu_{F}_{(C)} = \mu \circ F(\mu)$

$\mu \circ \eta = 1 = \mu \circ F(\eta)$

** Adjoints give Monads

Let $F \perp G$ be an adjoint pair. Then $(G \circ F, \mu, \eta)$ is a monad.

Dually, $(F \circ G, \Delta , \varepsilon  )$ forms a comonad
