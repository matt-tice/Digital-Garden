* Unbounded Operators

We diagonalize the self-adjoint operator as
\[
A = \int \lambda dE(\lambda)
\]
where $E$ is not a scalar measure, but is rather a map
\[
E: \mathcal{B}(\sigma(A)) \rightarrow P(H)
\]
where $\mathcal{B}(\sigma(A))$ is the Borel sigma algebra on the spectrum, and $P(H)$ is the space of orthogonal projections on $H$.
