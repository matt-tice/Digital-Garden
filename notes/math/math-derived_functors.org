* Summary

1. Take an object (like a sheaf)
2. Take a resolution
3. Apply a functor (like taking global sections)
4. Take the cohomology of the resulting complex
        - this step loses a lot of information
        - if you want to keep that info, you look at [[id:c425042c-5f39-44db-953f-8aedfda7e2ba][derived categories]]

What you get out the other side is a *derived functor*.
