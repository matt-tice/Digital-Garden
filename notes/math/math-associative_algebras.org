* Definitions

** Finitely generated algebra

Let \( R \) be a commutative ring. An \( R \)-algebra \( A \) is *finitely generated* if there is a surjective morphism of algebras
\[
R[x_{1}, \ldots , x_{n}] \rightarrow A
\]
for some \( n. \)



* Theorems

** Finitely generated are quotients

An algebra \( A \) is finitely generated if and only if 
\[
A \cong R[x_{1} , \ldots , x_{n}] / I
\]

*** Proof

If \( A \cong R[x_{1} , \ldots , x_{n}] / I \) then \( R[x_{1}, \ldots , x_{n}] \rightarrow R[x_{1}, \ldots , x_{n}] / I  \) is a surjection.

And if we have a surjection \( \phi : R[x_{1}, \ldots , x_{n}] \rightarrow A \), then apply the first isomorphism theorem to get 
\[
R[x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}] / \ker \phi \cong Im \phi  = A
\]
