* Summary

When you do ordinary (co)limits in Top, replacing your spaces by homotopy equivalent ones changes the value of the (co)limit to a different homotopy class in general.

The pushout of $S^{n} \rightarrow *$ is $S^{n}^{}^{+}^{1}$

The coequalizer becomes the mapping torus

The coequalizer of a single morphism $X \rightarrow Y$ is the mapping cyliner

* Definitions

** Derived Category Version

The homotopy colimit is the left derived functor of the ordinary colimit functor

** Bar Construction

Inside of Top, you can use the bar construction to get homotopy (co)limits.

** Quasicategories

Inside of a quasicategory, a homotopy (co)limit is just the ordinary colimit in that setting
