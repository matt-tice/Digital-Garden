* Swing

Swing music was massive and for the masses. It was played in orchestras in ballrooms where people were expected to dance.

=Diatonic:= Focused on triads and sixths.

=Call and response:= 

=Little improv:= Because the orchestras were so large, there was little room for improvisation. There would be designated soloists, and they typically only got eight or sixteen bars.
