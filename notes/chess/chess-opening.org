* Center control

One of the reasons the center is so important is because it's the point of *symmetry breaking* on the board. 

** Attacks in the center

Whenever the opponent plays on the flank, you should try to respond in the center. For example *a3, h3, a6, h6* are all flank moves

** Bishops and knights

** Doubled

You want to avoid doubled pawns

* Bishops

Develop them *outside of the pawn chain* 

* Rooks

Go on the files that you expect to open

* Closed positions

Don't close the position down arbitrarily

In these positions, you want to *take space* and use *prophylaxis* 

* Open positions

Speed is imperative in these positions. Taking back pawns is not a good use of time --- *develop your pieces*
