* Pawns

** Triangles

*** Don't allow the sideways triangle 

For example, pawns on e3, f4, e5

*** Don't attack the northern triangle 
facing away

*** Do attack the southern triangle

Attack triangles facing towards you

** Attack the half isolated pawn

** Doubled pawns

*** Are strong in the center

** f3

*** Conditions for playing

Play it only if your opponent's dark bishop and queen are blocked in by both pawns

** Skipped pawns

If there are two pawns with an open file between them, the pawns are *very vulnerable* 

** Don't push the pawn past your rook

If you're defending a pawn from the side with your rook, it's extremely dangerous to push it further

** Passed pawns

*** Skewer to stop it

One way to stop a passed pawn is to skewer it to another piece

* Skewers

** Allow your opponent to take a pawn, then skewer

* Modern opening

Two pawns in the center and then flank pawns

* Geometry

** Two pieces diagonally

=Knight:= Can fork them 

=Bishop:= Can skewer them 

** Diagonal in front of king

Can be used to cut the king off; for example, from castling

* Don't attack into a pawn

Don't offer to trade pieces, if the opponent can block you with a pawn, gaining tempo

* Checks

Bring your queen closer into the attack whenever you check with it
