* Battery Life

mpv avoids having to process DOM scripts, trackers, CSS, etc. This actually saves battery life and cpu usage

* yt-dlp

mpv integrates neatly with yt-dlp out of the box. You can pass a url to mpv, which it will pass to yt-dlp, who resolves the web stream and then passes it back to mpv, who pipes it to your media playback engine.

As a consequence, mpv ends up streaming the file to you, rather than waiting for it all to download
