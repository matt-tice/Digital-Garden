* Dryness

=Inflammation:= When dry air desiccates the nasal lining, the *blood vessels dilate*.

=Pressue:= The extra blood increases the pressure, causing plasma fluid to leak through the vessel walls into the surrounding tissue and surface of the nasal passage, moisturizing it.

=Temperature:= The influx of warm blood heats up the air entering the nose

=Goblet cells:= Are stimulated to ramp up production of watery secretions

=Paracellular water transport:= This increases, as tiny channels between cells are opened, to allow water move directly from deeper tissues onto the exposed epithelial surface
