* Summary

* Saturated Fats

These are /saturated with hydrogen/ 

Their chains lay flat, allowing them to pack together into *solids at room temperature.* 

* Unsaturated Fats

These fats have chains with kinks in them, from the missing hydrogen. This makes them *less stable* at room temperature; they *go rancid* 

** Mono

Have one missing hydrogen pair. This leads them to be liquid at room temperature and thick when chilled.



** Poly

These are liquid at room temperature and when chilled

Used to build the membranes of your brain cells.

This group contains *essential fatty acids*, which the body *can't produce* on its own.

* Trans Fats

These are mainly artificial fats produced by pumping hydrogen into vegetable oils to make them solid (to make them more stable at room temperature)

ex. Margarine
