* Resting Metabolic Rate

Surprisingly, your /RMR per pound of muscle/ remains basically the same from age 20 to 60

Your RMR is highly correlated with your muscle mass --- the more muscle you have, the higher your RMR

* Sertraline

Can reduce *non-exercise acivity thermogenesis* (NEAT)
- which is to say, fidgeting, pacing, etc

Does not impact RMR
Does impact your satiation response

* Sedentarity

** Spontaneous Physical Activity

You can intentionally do NEAT movements

*** Soleus Muscle

It runs from the back of the knee to the heel

Highly resistant to fatigue

Rapid heel raises while keeping toes planted and sitting
- rest forearms or weight on your knees


*** Postural Oscillated Shift

Shift your weight from one sit-bone to the other

* Isometric Exercise

Holding a muscle under tension forces the muscle to expend energy rapidly

** Posture

Good posture is a form of isometric exercise
- it uses the *axial stabilizer network*


** Toe Walking

Puts a continuous load on your calves

Leads to Type I (slow-twitch muscle fibers)
- fatigue resistant
- mitochondria-rich
