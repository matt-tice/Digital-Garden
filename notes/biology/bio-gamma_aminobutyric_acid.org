#+title: gamma-aminobutyric acid
#+filetags: :biology:brain:gaba:neurotransmitter:

* References

* Summary

Calms the nervous system

Balances with [[id:2aebf5bf-2284-49b4-a4fb-7e5e9161f891][Glutamic Acid / Glutamate]]

* Monophasic

Like glutamate, GABA is monophasic: the more of it you have, the more it does; in this case that means it inhibits you more and more
- can eventually cause coma, and brain death
- it's intentionally used for clinical anesthesia

* Drugs

These bind to GABA receptors, making your brain hyper-sensitive to them

** Valium

** Xanax

** Ambien

** Alcohol

Mimics GABA in the brain
