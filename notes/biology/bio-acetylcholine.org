* Biphasic Roles

** Tonic

Activates nicotinic receptors and muscarinic receptors to sharpen sensory processing and sustain wakefulness

** Spiked

Activates muscarinic autoreceptors M_2 and M_4. This produces a negative feedback loop that shuts down further acetylcholine release
