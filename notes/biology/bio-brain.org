** Cerebrum

At the top / front of your head
The largest part of the brain.

Cerebral cortex: the outer surface of the cerebrum. Very wrinkly, with 2 hemispheres
- Corpus callosum: connects the hemispheres

Hemisphers have 5 lobes each:
1. frontal
   - largest individual lobe
   - reasoning
   - voluntary movement
   - executive function
   - social convention
   - learning / recalling info
   
2. parietal (top)
3. temporal (side)
4. insular (inside)
5. occipital (back)
