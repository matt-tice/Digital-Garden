* Ketosis

You need low carbs

You can actually trigger ketosis with vegetarian and vegans diets without much difficulty.

* Foods

** Fats

- Extra virgin olive oil
- coconut oil
- MCT oil
- nuts
- seeds

** Proteins

- tofu
- tempeh
- seitan
- lupini beans
- pea or hemp protein

* Keto Diet

A diet with *moderate protein* and *high fat* 

If you eat too much protein, you'll actually get *kicked out of ketosis*; the liver will take that protein and convert it through gluconeogenesis

If you just eat processed meats, you'll end up taking in a lot of saturated fats and sodium and not have enough fiber

You really want things like salads
- spinach, kale, broccoli, cauliflower, in olive oil with avocado, nuts, and eggs
