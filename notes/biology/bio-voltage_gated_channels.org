#+title: voltage-gated channels
#+filetags: :biology:brain:protein:ion_channel:

* References

* Summary


At rest, a neuron has a negative charge (approximately $-70 mV$). Voltage-gated channels are shut at this voltage. When a neighbouring neuron sends it signal, the voltage is made a bit more positive.

If the voltage of a neuron reaches a certain threshold (usually $-55mV$), the voltage-gated channel opens.

* Sodium channels

Voltage-gated sodium channels open *very fast* once the threshold is reached. Sodium rushes into the cell, and the voltage spikes to $+30mV$

** Anesthetic

Lidocaine blocks voltage-gated sodium channels, preventing you from getting pain signals

* Potassium channels

Voltage-gated potassium channels open *with a slight delay*. Potassium rushes out of the cell, dropping the neuron to its resting negative state.
