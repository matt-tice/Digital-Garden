* Summary

Your stomach churns food for 2-4 hours before it passes on. It tries to turn liquify the chewed up food (called a *bolus*) into a uniform paste called *chyme.* 

It then goes to the [[id:503a8f58-b152-420b-a4b4-c2d202fe84b5][small intestine]]

* [[Id:7d45e4a7-7be7-49ed-acf9-dc6495008741][Ghrelin]]

The hunger hormone. It works on a regular clock. If you eat every day at the same time, it will make you feel hungry around that time each day.

If you don't eat, eventually the ghrelin will stop rising and drop back down after about an hour
