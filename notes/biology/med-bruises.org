Bruises are caused by broken [[id:b3fc3adb-e5bb-4dcf-bc6c-d89380d227ed][capillaries]], usually due to blunt trauma
When you see a bruise, you're seeing the blood spilling out into the surrounding tissue

Soft tissues get bigger bruises
- the blood can travel outwards easier

problems with coagulation/clotting allows more blood to flow, making larger bruises
