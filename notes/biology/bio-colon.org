* Summary

The large intestine (aka the colon) *does not produce enzymes*. Its role is to *absorb water.* It also houses your *microbiome.* 

* Fiber

Your colon microbiome breaks down fiber through *fermentation*. This process produces hydrogen and methane gas. 

** Too much fiber

If you have too much fiber (for instance if you don't chew seeds and just swallow them whole) you get a ton of gas, causing the walls of the intestines to expand (*bloating*). This will trigger muscle spasms (*cramping*)
