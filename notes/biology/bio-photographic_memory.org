* Summary

* Eidetic Memory

You look at an image and after it's removed, you continue to see it not only in your mind's eye, but in your actual eyes. 
This ability is found in some kids, but typically disappears by adulthood.

* Hyperthymesia

A condition where you remember every day of your life. But you don't remember it visually; it's like you remember the facts; like a diary entry.
