* Glomeruli

Tiny blood vessels, that filter proteins from water and waste

- waste and water goes through



** Hyperfiltration

Eating too much protein stresses the kidneys by forcing them to process high levels of waste

Blood vessels dilate to increase blood flow to the kidneys, but this *increases the pressure in the glomeruli*
