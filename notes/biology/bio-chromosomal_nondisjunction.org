* Summary

Nondisjunction is the failure of homologous chromosomes or sister chromatids to separate properly during cell division. 

This leads to some cells having more chromosomes than usual and some having fewer

* Age

Risk of meiotic nondisjunction increases sharply with advanced maternal age: around age 30 to 35, and an exponential rise after age 40.

Over time the proteins that hold sister chromatids together gradually degrade

* Trisomy 21 (Down syndrome)

Caused by an extra full or partial copy of chromosome 21 due to nondisjunction. This is the most common cause of down syndrome (95%). Other causes are *translocation* (part of chromosome 21 attaches to a different chromosome) and *mosaicism* (some cells have an extra copy of chromosome 21 while others have the typical two copies)
