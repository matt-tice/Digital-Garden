* Summary

In the past, neurodivergent traits were seen as isolated features (ie you were autistic /or/ you had ADHD). Nowadays, researchers understand that if you're neurodivergent, you likely have a *bundle* of neurodivergent traits
        - this is sometimes called *multi-neurodivergent* or *complex neurovariant*


* ADHD

Characterized by *low tonic dopamine* 

** Rejection Sensitivite Dysphoria

** Reward Deficiency Syndrome

Characterized by *few D2 receptors* or low tonic dopamine

* Autism

Characterized by a *lack of synaptic pruning during childhood*. This can cause things like *synesthesia* 

People with autism or ADHD often have internal voices / internal committees that they engage in long, complex dialogue with

* Dissociative Identity Disorder

Frequent time loss

Internal arguing

Depersonalization

* Schizophrenia

Alters how your brain processes external data, making it difficult to tell if something is real or not

External voices (from the walls, a passing car, etc.) They speak with you, rather than arguing with each other

flat affect, social withdrawl, lack of motivation
