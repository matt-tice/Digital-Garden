* Oxytocin

During breastfeeding, oxytocin is rapidly released into the bloodstream. It acts on the mammary glands to squeeze the alveoli, so milk is let down into *lactiferous ducts* (this is called the *letdown reflex*)

Suckling at the nipple is relayed to the hypothalamus, which causes the brain to produce the oxytocin and the lactocyctes to produce milk

Oxytocin release has a positive feedback loop, which releases more oxytocin until the triggering event ceases
