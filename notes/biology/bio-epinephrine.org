** Peripheral sympathetic dominance
Imagine you have high adrenaline but low NE. Adrenaline can't cross the blood-brain barrier, so it will only influence your body; your body will be in *fight-or-flight*, but your mind will be calm --- your heart will be pounding, your breathing shallow, but your mind will not feel it, and may even feel *unfocused*
