* References


* Summary

Antibodies are another word for *immunoglobin*

Y-shaped

Part of the adaptive immune system

* Types

There are 5 isotypes of antibodies

** IgA

Has to do with immune system of mucous membranes. The IgA producing cells make up over 80% of all Ig-producing cells. But IgA only makes up about 15% of your antibodies

** IgD

25% of your antibodies

** IgE

Least abundant of the antibodies (0.05%)

Only found in mammals.
- made by *plasma cells*

Plays an important role in *type 1 hypersensitivity*  

** IgG

The most common antibody in *blood circulation*

Created and released by *plasma B cells*

** IgM

The largest antibody.

It appears first in response to an antigen.

Mainly created in the *spleen*
