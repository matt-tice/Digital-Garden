* Dual-Process Theory

The pyschologist Daniel Kahneman broke down thinking into System 1 and System 2:

1. Intuitive
2. Logical / calculating

This was popularized in his /Thinking Fast and Slow/ 

Explicit memory relies on the *prefrontal cortex* (working memory) and the *hippocampus* (memories).

System 1 relies more on the *basal ganglia* and *cerebellum* 

** Myelination

Once axons are wrapped in myelin, the electrical signals along them speed up by up to 100 times
- this lets the intuition be instaneous


** Children's Brains

Filled with acetylcholine, which triggers myelination
