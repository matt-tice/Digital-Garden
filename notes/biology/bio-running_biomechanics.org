#+title: running biomechanics
#+filetags: :biology:body:fitness:

* References

* Summary

* Body

** Angle

Your body should be slightly leaning forward /from the ankles, not the waist/
- gravity pulls you forward 

** Feet

*Do not heel-strike when running*
- the impact is too high

Land on the *ball of your foot*, /underneath your hips/

** Arms

Bend your elbows at 90 deg; drive your elbows forward and back

Keep your shoulders relaxed
