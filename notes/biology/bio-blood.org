* Tests


** Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Measures all the different blood cells

1. Red blood cells
   carry oxygen
2. White blood cells
   fight infections
3. Platelets
   stop bleeding by clotting
4. Hemoglobin
   iron-rich protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen
5. Hematocrit
   amount of whole blood made up of red blood cells
6. Mean corpuscular volume
   size of red blood cells


** Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate (ESR)

Sedimentation rate: time for red blood cells to settle at the bottom of a test tube

[[id:89db38a0-0c02-4ab0-aee5-a9b526431852][inflammation]] causes red blood cells to clump together, making them fall to the bottom of the tube faster

* Hemostasis



** Coagulation



*** Clots (Thrombi)

Made up of blood parts called *cruor* (mainly red blood cells and fibrin, along with a few platelets)

The platelets and red blood cells form a plug that can block the veins or arteries.

The fibrin forms a mesh

Thrombi are actually natural parst of injury response; they stop further bleeding. 

**** Thrombosis

When a clot obstructs a blood vessel. 

When it impacts the lungs, we're looking at a [[id:850acfa5-3f74-409b-a457-69776905936d][pulmonary embolism]]
