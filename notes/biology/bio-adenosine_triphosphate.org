#+title: adenosine triphosphate

* Summary

The three phosphate groups all have a negative charge, and repel each other

ATP is converted into adenosive *diphosphate*, by breaking one of the phosphate groups off. This ADP is then recycled by reattaching the third phosphate groups, using the energy you get from food.
