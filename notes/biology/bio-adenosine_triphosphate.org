* References

* Summary

The three phosphate groups all have a negative charge, and repel each other

ATP is converted into adenosine *diphosphate*, by breaking one of the phosphate groups off. This ADP is then recycled by reattaching the third phosphate groups, using the energy you get from food.

* Cyclic Adenosine Monophosphate

Made from ATP by *adenylyl cyclase*, which strips off two phosphates and curls the AMP into a ring, releasing *pyrophosphate*
