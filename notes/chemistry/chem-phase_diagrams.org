* Triple Point

The P and T where the substance exists in all 3 phases. These are called the triple point temperature (T_tp) and triple point pressure (P_tp)

Helium is the only substance with no triple point at 1 atm

At temperatures below T_c, your substance *cannot be a liquid*
- it can still be a solid or gas, however
