* Inhaling

Unlike in movies, it would actually take several minutes of continuous inhalation to induce anesthesia

Also the dosage difference between being knocked out and being killed is tiny

* Teflon

Chloroform is used to produce chlorodifluoromethane, which is the main compound used in making Teflon! (for non-stick cookware)
