#+title: random walks

* References

Local limit theorems for random isometries of the plane, Drogin and Hernández.

* Summary


Random walks on planar isometries
- you consider the group G of isometries on, say, R^2
- you place a measure on the group with finite support
- and draw elements independently and uniformly from the measure
