Definitions

Local sections of a bundle

Let π : E → M be a fiber bundle. A local section of the bundle is a pair (U, f), where U ⊆ M is an open set and f : U → E is a section of the bundle: π ∘ f = idU

Global versus local

Global sections are the case where U = M. Such sections don't always exist, which is why local section are useful.

Jet equivalence on a fiber bundle

Let π : E → M be a smooth fiber bundle, and let Γ(p) be the space of local sections whose domains contain p ∈ M.

Two smooth sections f, g ∈ Γ(p) are k-equivalent if all of their partial derivatives up to order k are equal at p. In particular, f(p) = g(p).

Jets: The equivalence class of local sections under the jet equivalence is called a k-jet, and is denoted jpk. Given f ∈ Γ(p), we denote the equivalence class of this element by jpkf.

Jet bundle

Let E → M be a fiber bundle, and let p ∈ M. The kth jet manifold of π is the set Jk(π) = {jpkf ∣ p ∈ M, f ∈ Γ(p)}
