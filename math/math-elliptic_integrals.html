Summary

Elliptic integrals are integrals of the form ∫(p(x))−1/2, where p is a polynomial of deg(p) = 3 or 4

The inverses of elliptic integrals are elliptic curves

They're also related to tori

Elliptic Curves and Functions

Elliptic curves are not the same as elliptic functions

Elliptic integrals, when undergoing inversion gives us a differential equation which defines an elliptic curve. The solution to the differential equation is a an elliptic function, which then parameterizes the elliptic curve.

Sigma and Theta Functions

Just like how sin x and cos x arise out of a single function eix, elliptic functions arise out of sigma and theta functions.
