Summary

Presheafs

Presheafs are basically generalizations of right modules. So a presheaf F : 𝒞op → Set can be thought of as a right representation of 𝒞, or as a right 𝒞-module structure on Set, where the action of an "element" (ie a morphism) f is f ⋅ x = F(f)(x) for all x ∈ F(f)s.

In the case that we take functors BG → Vec, we get ordinary representation theory of groups, as left k[G] -modules. If we do it with presheaves, we get right modules.

This further implies that topos theory is a generalization of representation theory.

Yoneda Embedding is Fully Faithful

Consider the Yoneda embedding h− : 𝒞 → Set𝒞op. Applying the Yoneda lemma, we get Nat (hA,hB) ≅ Mor (A,B). Since the isomorphism in the category of sets is a bijection, this tells us that the Yoneda embedding is fully faithful.

Uses

Proving Isomorphisms

Often instead of proving A ≅ B directly, you can just prove that Mor(X,A) ≅ Mor(X,B) for all X, naturally. In other words, you show hA ≅ hB.

Let hA ≅ hB, witnessed by the natural transformation η. Consider ηA: Mor(A, A) → Mor(A, B) and ηB-1: Mor(B, B) → Mor(B, A). Now we take the diagram

Mor(A, A) → Mor(B, A) ηA ↓ ↓ ηB Mor(A, B) → Mor(B, B)

Mor(f, A) = - ˆ f, for any f: B → A. Now taking ηB-1(1B) as our f, we have ηA(1A) ∘ ηB−1(1B) = (Mor(ηB−1(1B),B)∘ηA)[1A] = (ηB∘Mor(ηB−1(1B),A))[1A] = ηB ∘ 1A ∘ ηB−1 = 1B. Similarly, ηB−1(1B) ∘ ηA(1A) = (Mor(ηA(1A),A)∘ηB−1)[1B] = ηA−1 ∘ (Mor(ηA(1A),B))[1B] = ηA−1 ∘ 1B ∘ ηA(1A) = 1A. Thus ηA(1A) and ηB-1(1B) are inverse pairs, showing that A ≅ B.

The yoneda embedding h− is fully faithful. We have hA ≅ hB. Then by the fact that fully faithful functors reflect isomorphisms, we get that A ≅ B.

Grothendieck Topology

Use the Yoneda embedding to send A → hA. Then inside of the presheaf category, all limits and colimits exist. You perform the (co)limit there, and then check whether your result is representable. If it is, you drop back down into the original category.
