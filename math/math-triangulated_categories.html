Summary

When you take a short exact sequence of chain complexes, the homology groups end up forming a long exact sequence

Definitions

Triangulated Category

Additive category T

Additive translation functor: T → T

  • this plays the role of a suspension

Collection of exact triangles X → Y → Z → X[1]

  • these play the roles of short exact sequences
  • as well as fiber sequences and cofiber sequences

We'll denote these triangles by [X,Y,Z] or [XYZ]

The triangles satisfy

TR 1

For every object X, we have an exact triangle [XX0] given by X X  → 0 → X[1]

TR 2

For every f: X → Y there is an exact triangle [XYZ] X Y → Z → X[1]

TR 3

[XYZ] is an exact triangle if and only if [YZX[1]] is

X Y → Z → X[1] Y → Z → X[1] Y[1]

TR 4 (Octahedral Axiom)

Given three triangles of a certain form, there exists another triangle such that they all satisfy a big diagram
