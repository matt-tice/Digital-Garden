Math

If P is the price of the underlying asset, the derivative price is V(P,t) ∂ V/ ∂ P = Δ is the "delta"

∂2P V is gamma.

High gamma means serious price spikes or crashes

∂t V is theta

  • it tells you about how the derivative loses value, even if the price of the underlying commodity is fixed

Alpha is the return that isn't explained by the market

  • it's the actual skill of the trader

Beta is a measure of how your system correlates with the market

  • β = 2 means that you're amplifying the market's movement by 200%
