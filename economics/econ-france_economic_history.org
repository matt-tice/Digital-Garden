#+title: france economic history

* References 

(2004) Jean-Pierre Dormois, The French Economy in the Twentieth Century

* Summary 

Demographic transition: France saw its population stagnate between the 1800s and 1900s for nearly 100 years

Malthusian: Intentionally limiting family size
- designed to preserve property holdings, since the family estate would be divided equally between children
- prior to WW2, single-child families were very common
- because of this, France fell behind in production
